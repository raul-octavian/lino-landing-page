Everything is on the table: linoleum blocks, carving tools, inks, a press and good paper. Bring an idea and an apron.

“The first print is never the good one. The third usually is. ”

Ida Holm has printed linocuts for fifteen years and teaches the way she wishes she’d been taught: slowly, in small groups, with plenty of ink on her sleeves.

Seat number 09
13–14 March Atelier Nord

Good to know.

Do I need any experience?

No. Most of the group has never held a carving tool, and the weekend is paced for that.

What should I bring?

An apron and an idea. We provide blocks, tools, inks, paper and the press.

What if I can’t make it?

Move to the next weekend, or get a full refund up to 14 days before.

Is lunch included?

Coffee and snacks are, and there is a long lunch break with good places nearby.

Lino Weekend is a concept project. The studio, teacher and details are fictional.
